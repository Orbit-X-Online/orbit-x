How Orbit-X works

Orbit-X is a helper that tells your family when you need help. It also remembers your blood pressure and your tablets. It is not a doctor. It never tells you what medicine to take.

Who is who?

Me (the person who needs looking after). I use the main app.
My caregiver (a child, a friend, a neighbour). Their phone gets an alert when I need help.
My doctor or nurse (optional). They write simple alert rules, like "tell me if I report a headache and blurry eyes". I read every rule before it starts.
A clinic or hospital. They get a special area for connecting their software. A normal person never sees it.

If you are the person (5 taps)

1
Tap "I want to look after my own health"
Type your name. Tick the times you take tablets. Tap Start.
2
Tap Family, then add a caregiver
Type their name (and phone number if you like). Tap Add and get invite.
3
Send them the invite on WhatsApp
The invite works one time, on one phone. If it fails, tap New invite.
4
Tap Settings, then "Turn on phone alerts"
Now I can remind you about tablets, even when the app is closed.
5
Tap "Log BP" and say or type your reading
For example: "150 over 95". After 3 days I learn what is normal for you.

If you are the caregiver (3 taps)

1
Open the invite link and tap Join
2
Tap "Arm this phone"
3
Tap Allow when the phone asks about notifications
Done. Your phone now gets an alert even when Orbit-X is closed. Tap "Send me a test alert" to check.

What happens in an emergency?

1. A very high reading, a scary word like "chest pain", or the red I need help now button starts the alert. Plain code decides this. No AI.

2. The first caregiver's phone makes a notification and rings. They have 20 seconds.

3. No answer? The next caregiver is alerted. Our server does this by itself, even if your phone is off.

4. If nobody answers, you are told to call 112 and you get big Call and Text buttons for each caregiver.

Tablet reminders

At your tablet time I remind you. If you still have not tapped "I took my tablet" a few hours later, your caregivers get a gentle message. You can turn this off in Settings.

Where do I add documents?

Open My records (the Health Workspace), then the Documents tab.

• Paste the words from a lab report or hospital paper into the box, or choose a .txt file. Then tap Analyse.
• Or tap Load the synthetic samples to try it with pretend papers.

Orbit finds the numbers, shows exactly which words they came from, and compares reports over time. It only works with text for now (no photos or PDFs yet).

What Orbit-X never does

• It never diagnoses an illness.
• It never tells you to start, stop or change a medicine.
• It never lets an AI decide an emergency.
• It always shows where a fact came from.

Orbit-X is a hackathon project and not a medical device. In an emergency, call 112.

Just looking? (demo)

On the first page, the box called Just want to look around? has a one-minute story and a pretend patient. Nothing in it is real, and nobody is contacted.
