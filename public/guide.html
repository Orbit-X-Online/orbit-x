How to use Orbit-X

Orbit-X reads health papers and shows you what they say. It always shows where each fact came from. It never gives medical advice. Everything here is pretend (synthetic) data, so you cannot break anything.

Part 1 · Read my health papers (1 minute)

1
Open the app and tap the blue button
Tap Try it with a synthetic patient. This makes a pretend patient called Amara.
2
Tap "Health workspace", then "Load the 3 synthetic documents"
Orbit reads two lab reports and a hospital paper and pulls out the numbers.
3
Look and ask
Tap Compare Reports to see what changed. Tap Ask My Records, press a question button, and tap Read aloud to hear the answer.

Open the health workspace

Part 2 · Connect hospital software (30 seconds)

1
Open the Developer area
It is for apps that talk to Orbit-X.
2
Tap "Run the demo for me"
Orbit does every step by itself: a pretend hospital sends a record, an emergency app asks for help, a receiver gets it, and the receipt is checked.
3
Tap "Open the public receipt"
The receipt proves nothing was changed. It shows no private details.

Open the Developer area

Good to know

• Pretend parts: the hospital and the emergency receiver are simulated. Nobody real is contacted.

• No medical advice: Orbit only shows what is written in the papers and says what is missing.

• Medicine times: in the main app, Settings lets you pick Morning, Afternoon, Evening and Night.

• Stuck? Go back to the first page and start again. Your pretend patient stays saved on this phone.
