What Orbit-X is, and is not

Orbit-X is a hackathon prototype that helps you keep a record of blood pressure readings and tablets, and tells the family members you choose when a reading is very high or low. It gives information only. It does not diagnose, give medical advice, or replace a doctor. It is not a medical device.

In an emergency call 112 (or your local emergency number). Do not wait for the app.

What we store

  • What you enter: your first name, blood pressure and pulse readings, tablet names and times you set, symptoms you type, and documents you choose to upload.
  • Family contacts you invite, and alerts sent to them.
  • Your data is kept in a private storage area for your household on Cloudflare. Other households cannot see it.
  • Your sign-in token and language choice are kept on your own phone. Keep your recovery key safe: it is how you sign back in.

Who may see it

  • Family members and caregivers you invite can see what the app shows them, including alerts.
  • Doctors or clinics only if you connect them. Emergency information handoff is off until you switch it on in the workspace, and you choose which categories are shared.
  • AI reading of documents: when you upload a document and ask Orbit-X to read or explain it, the text of that document is sent to an AI service (Baseten, running a Meta model) to be turned into plain words. Do not upload anything you are not comfortable sending. Answers are checked by safety rules, and the AI is not used for diagnosis or advice.
  • Sign-up check: if the site owner switches on the "quick check that you are a person" (Cloudflare Turnstile), your browser shares technical details with Cloudflare to run it.
  • Alert messages (push notification, and SMS or voice call where switched on) pass through the delivery services needed to reach the phone.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: in the app, open Settings and choose “Delete my account”. This removes your household data and cannot be undone.
  • Remove a caregiver at any time from the Family screen.
  • Sign out on a phone to remove the sign-in from that phone.
  • Testing is voluntary. If you do not agree, simply do not create an account.

Limits you should know about

  • This is a prototype. Alerts can fail or be delayed (phone settings, battery saving, no signal). Never rely on Orbit-X as your only way to get help.
  • No regulatory approval or compliance certification is claimed.
  • Please use fictional or low-risk information if you only want to try the app.

Your consent

By creating an account or continuing to use Orbit-X you confirm that you have read this page, that you are choosing to share the information described above, and that you have the permission of anyone whose details you add.
