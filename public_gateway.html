Orbit-X Cuff Gateway

Turns any phone or laptop with Chrome into a bridge: a Bluetooth blood-pressure cuff measures, and every reading goes straight into the patient's Orbit-X record for the doctor to see. No typing.

1. Connect to the patient's account

Make a key on the doctor page: Connect, "Send readings and alerts". It stays on this device only.

2. Connect the cuff

Switch the cuff on, then choose it from the list. Needs Chrome on Android or a computer, with Bluetooth on.

3. Measure

Take a measurement on the cuff. It is sent automatically.

- / -

Many cheap cuffs only work with their own app and cannot connect here. If yours does not, the page tells you what it offers.
